HardPix

A radiation spectrometer

Institute of Experimental and Applied Physics · Czech Technical University in Prague

Three standalone pages. Each one loads the instrument's real CAD geometry and takes it apart in the browser.

The pages First load
  1. 01 The film

    Fourteen Millimetres→

    Sixty-four seconds of the instrument in real time, ending on real particle tracks measured by a Timepix2 chip. It plays once, under a score, then hands the model over to you. The title is the width of the sensitive silicon — 256 pixels of 55 micrometres.     94 MB 64 seconds · sound
  2. 02 Exploded view

    HardPix SWIMMR→

    A scroll-driven teardown of the SWIMMR unit over five screens, built before the film. Nearly as heavy as the film, because it fetches an 8K sky alongside the model. It carries known faults — the measured data lands about 8 mm off on the silicon, and its colour scale runs backwards, so the faintest hits read hottest. Neither is fixed.     75 MB Scroll to explode
  3. 03 Exploded view

    An Earlier Assembly→

    The same scroll-driven treatment over five screens, applied to an earlier assembly. The oldest of the three, and by some distance the lightest — the one to open first on a slow connection.     24 MB Scroll to explode

Each figure is everything the page fetches before it can draw its first frame — geometry and measured data, not video. Worth knowing if you are on mobile data.

The first HardPix launched 12 June 2023 — Falcon 9, a D-Orbit ION spacecraft, UKRI SWIMMR-1.
