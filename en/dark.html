Handcrafted wall word clocks that uniquely display time in words in Czech or English. Each piece is an original, carefully crafted with attention to detail. The clocks not only accurately measure time but also become a distinctive design element of any interior. Ideal for those seeking a combination of functionality, originality, and modern style.

The lettering is lit by LEDs at 5500 K. Brightness is adjustable across five levels, using the buttons on the back of the clock or the web app.

An internal RTC with a backup battery keeps the clock running. It holds the time even when unplugged — several years on the backup battery — so neither a power cut nor a move means setting the clock again. The clock can also be connected to your home Wi-Fi network.

The usual build time is three weeks from order. If anything goes wrong with the clock, the repair is free of charge.
